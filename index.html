Open source · Legal data · v1.14

JITS Legal

Indian court judgments, turned into clean, searchable data. JITS reads each judgment and pulls out the court, date, cited cases and the laws it invokes, including the 2024 switch from IPC/CrPC to BNS/BNSS.

There's no AI guessing involved. Every field comes from a written rule, so running it twice gives exactly the same result.

Research · Particle physics

Hidden Vertex

The Large Hadron Collider throws away almost all of its collision data, keeping only events that match what physicists expect to see. Anything truly new could be lost.

Hidden Vertex trains a graph neural network on ordinary collisions only, so anything it can't explain stands out. Part 1 showed that looking at particles one by one isn't enough to spot new physics, which sets up Part 2.

Winner · TechXpression 2025

Sentinel

Real-time fraud detection for UPI and bank payments. Two fast rule checks catch the obvious attacks first; a neural network only looks at what gets past them. That keeps a rare but genuine purchase from being flagged as fraud.

Hi, I'm Jamil.

I'm a Computer Engineering student who likes building things that hold up under scrutiny.

Checking them, and knowing what to throw away, is where the real thinking happens. That's what draws me to agent systems: they give software room for a second opinion.
